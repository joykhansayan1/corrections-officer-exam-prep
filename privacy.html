Privacy Policy

App: Corrections Officer Exam Prep (iPhone, iPad and Android)
Developer: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Corrections Officer Exam Prep (“the app,” “we,” “us”) is designed to protect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

1. We do not collect personal data

The app does not require an account, login, email, or profile. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information. The app contains no advertising software and no third-party analytics software, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

2. Your study data stays on your device

Your progress — answers, scores, streaks, bookmarks, flagged questions, spaced-repetition schedule, mock-exam history, exam date, study goal, and settings — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us. Deleting the app, or using Settings → Reset all progress, permanently removes this data.

3. Purchases

Subscriptions and the one-time Lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google Play on Android. The store handles payment and receipt validation under its own privacy policy (Apple, Google). We do not receive your name, payment details, Apple ID, or Google account. The app only checks, on-device, whether an active purchase exists in order to unlock features.

4. Reminders and read-aloud

If you turn on the optional daily study reminder, the app schedules a local notification on your device at the time you choose; Android asks for notification permission when you do. The optional read-aloud feature uses your device’s built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is sent to any server.

5. No ads, no tracking

The app shows no ads and uses no advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking. It is declared as collecting no data on its App Store privacy label and in its Google Play Data safety section.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a study tool intended for adults preparing for employment exams and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. You control all app data on your device and can erase it at any time via Settings → Reset all progress, or by deleting the app. We do not “sell” or “share” personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

8. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and post the new version on this page.
