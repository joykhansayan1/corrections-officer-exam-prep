How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Open the app and go to Settings → Restore Purchases (Restore also appears on the unlock screen). Use the same Apple ID (iPhone/iPad) or Google account (Android) you bought with.

How do I cancel or manage my subscription?

On iPhone and iPad: Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. On Android: open the Google Play app, tap your profile icon, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Is there a free trial?

Yes — the annual plan includes a 3-day free trial for eligible new subscribers. You won’t be charged if you cancel before the trial ends.

What does the app include?

2,000+ original questions across reading comprehension, situational judgment, memory & observation, grammar & writing, basic math, report interpretation and map reading; a full-length timed mock exam scored at 70%; 18 study guides and a glossary; flashcards; spaced-repetition review; and progress tracking. It works fully offline.

How do I set or change my daily reminder?

Go to Settings → Daily reminder and choose a time. On Android, allow notifications when asked.

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings → Reset all progress. All data is stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
